Key Insights from Clustering

1. ETA Creep & Volatility is the largest observed friction theme: 47 of the 100 analyzed 1-star reviews were classified under ETA Creep & Volatility. This makes delivery-time predictability the most prominent issue in this review sample.

"App promised 25 mins. After 20 mins, it silently pushed the ETA to 45 mins. Total wait was 70 mins! Don't lie on the live tracker." — Bhavna Sethi (51 helpful)

2. Repeated ETA changes appear to be a recurring user complaint: Several reviews describe delivery estimates increasing after the initial promise, with users asking for clearer and more honest delivery expectations.

"ETA is a total joke. Incremental delays every 5 minutes create so much anxiety. Give an honest range!" — Preeti Oberoi (47 helpful)

Root Cause Hypotheses: Why Might ETA Fail?

The review data identifies the customer-facing symptom, but does not reveal Swiggy's internal systems. The following are hypotheses that could explain the observed ETA complaints and should be validated with product and operational data.

1. ETA Prediction (Product / Tech)

Hypothesis: Delivery estimates may become less accurate when restaurant preparation time, rider availability, or order volume changes after the initial ETA is shown.

2. Restaurant Preparation (Operations)

Hypothesis: Unexpected increases in restaurant preparation time may create a gap between the initial ETA and the actual delivery time.

3. Delay Communication (UX)

Hypothesis: When delivery expectations change without a clear explanation or proactive update, users may perceive the experience as unreliable.

Executive 1-Pager Ready for Leadership Review

3. Proposed Solution: Confidence-Window ETA + Proactive Communication

  • Confidence-Window Display: Replace single-minute countdowns such as "Arriving in 32 min" with a dynamic range such as "Expected between 28–36 min" when delivery uncertainty is high.
  • Proactive Delay Communication: If the expected delivery window changes materially, notify the customer with the updated range and a brief explanation of the delay.
  • Kitchen Rush Signal: Explore a lightweight restaurant-side signal that can communicate unusually high preparation demand and improve ETA estimation.

4. Experiment & Success Metrics

Hypothesis: If users receive a delivery-time range that better reflects uncertainty, ETA accuracy and customer trust will improve without materially increasing cancellations or reducing checkout conversion.

  • Primary Metric: Percentage of orders delivered within the communicated ETA window.
  • Secondary Metric: ETA-related customer support contacts.
  • Secondary Metric: Post-delivery customer satisfaction.
  • Secondary Metric: Order cancellation rate.
  • Guardrail: Checkout conversion.
  • Guardrail: Merchant and rider operational metrics.

5. Trade-offs & Strategic Risks

Perceived Speed vs Honesty: Showing a range (e.g., 35–45 min) instead of 30 min may slightly depress checkout conversion. Mitigation: Maintain standard median ETA on the merchant discovery feed, and introduce the confidence range exclusively on the post-checkout tracking screen.

6. Feature Prioritization

The solution is prioritized based on expected customer impact, implementation effort, and dependency on operational changes.

7. A/B Test Design

Hypothesis: Replacing false-precision ETAs with confidence windows will improve perceived transparency and ETA accuracy without materially hurting checkout conversion.

Control: "Arriving in 32 minutes"

Variant: "Expected between 28–36 minutes"

  • Primary: Percentage of orders delivered within the communicated ETA window.
  • Secondary: ETA-related support contacts.
  • Secondary: Post-delivery customer satisfaction.
  • Guardrail: Checkout conversion rate.
  • Guardrail: Order cancellation rate.

Decision rule: Continue the variant if ETA accuracy improves while checkout conversion, cancellations, and customer satisfaction do not show a meaningful deterioration.
